# Texture credits

All planet, Sun, and ring textures in this folder are from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: all maps are re-encoded as WebP. Planet maps in this folder are resized from
2048×1024 to 1024×512 for the overview; `2k/` holds full-size planet maps, loaded for
the planet view. The Sun is resized to 1536×768.

## Pluto

`pluto.webp` and `2k/pluto.webp` are from NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics
Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute's New Horizons global color mosaic of Pluto
(public domain), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pluto_color_mapmosaic.jpg).
Changes: resized, re-encoded as WebP, shifted half a turn so longitude −180° is at the left edge, and the
unimaged (black) southern region lifted to a dark neutral.

## Moons

`moons/moon.webp` is Solar System Scope's Moon map (CC BY 4.0, as above). The others are
public domain, from Wikimedia Commons:

- `io.webp`: [Io from Galileo and Voyager missions](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Io_from_Galileo_and_Voyager_missions.jpg) (NASA/JPL/USGS)
- `europa.webp`: [Europa Voyager GalileoSSI global mosaic](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Europa_Voyager_GalileoSSI_global_mosaic.jpg) (USGS/PDS)
- `ganymede.webp`: [Ganymede map NASA JPL Voyager](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ganymede_map_NASA_JPL_Voyager.jpg) (Caltech/JPL/USGS)
- `enceladus.webp`: [Enceladus Color Map](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Enceladus_Color_Map.jpg) (NASA/JPL-Caltech/Space Science Institute/LPI)
- `miranda.webp`: [Miranda map JPL USGS](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Miranda_map_JPL_USGS.jpg) (Caltech/JPL/USGS)
- `triton.webp`: [Triton map no grid](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Triton_map_no_grid.jpg) (NASA/JPL/USGS)
- `charon.webp`: [Charon map iau1803c](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charon_map_iau1803c.jpg) (NASA/JHUAPL/SwRI)

Changes: resized to 1024×512 and re-encoded as WebP. Io's map, which stops short of the
poles, is stretched to full height. Europa and Ganymede are gently warmed toward their true
color. Unimaged (black) regions of Miranda, Triton, and Charon are lifted to a dark shade of
each moon's average color. Titan has no map: it is drawn as its opaque orange haze.
