# Texture credits

All planet, Sun, and ring textures in this folder are from
[Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: all maps are re-encoded as WebP. Planet maps in this folder are resized from
2048×1024 to 1024×512 for the overview; `2k/` holds full-size planet maps, loaded for
the planet view. The Sun is resized to 1536×768.

## Pluto

`pluto.webp` and `2k/pluto.webp` are from NASA/Johns Hopkins University Applied Physics
Laboratory/Southwest Research Institute's New Horizons global color mosaic of Pluto
(public domain), via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pluto_color_mapmosaic.jpg).
Changes: resized, re-encoded as WebP, shifted half a turn so longitude −180° is at the left edge, and the
unimaged (black) southern region lifted to a dark neutral.
